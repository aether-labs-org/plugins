# Notice

The `discovery` skill adapts some interaction mechanisms from the `brainstorming` skill of the
Superpowers project (https://github.com/obra/superpowers), version 7.0.0, released under the MIT
License, Copyright (c) 2025 Jesse Vincent:

- one question per message, with a corrigible guess preferred over a question;
- a play back of what was understood, in short parts, ending with a request for corrections;
- an independent reviewer that reads only the written artifact.

The adaptation reverses one of its choices on purpose: where `brainstorming` avoids showing
options while it is still learning the intent, this skill puts alternatives, trade-offs and market
references in the open. No text from the original skill is copied, and this plugin neither
depends on it nor calls it.
