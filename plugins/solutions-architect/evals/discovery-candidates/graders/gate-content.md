The framing document has all of: the intent and why now; goals, non-goals and anti-goals (the
Kubernetes anti-goal is present); the current state; constraints classified as must or prefer;
a `Source` marker (user, inferred, default or agent) on every item; two or three candidate
approaches plus one contrary option (buy, do nothing, extend what exists or something simpler);
discarded options with a one-line reason; discriminating questions that name a measurable
condition; a pre-mortem; a section for the independent challenge with its triage; and a
Discussion section that reads `Not held` with a reason and the questions that would have been
asked. Market references, if any, are marked `consulted` or `not consulted`. PASS only if all hold.
