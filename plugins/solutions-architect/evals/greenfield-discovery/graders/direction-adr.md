This ADR is the direction decision: it chooses among the candidate system-wide approaches (one of
them a contrary option such as buy, do nothing, extend what exists or something simpler),
scores them against criteria that cite NFR ids, and records its Discussion as `Not held` with a
reason and the questions that would have been asked. PASS only if all hold.
