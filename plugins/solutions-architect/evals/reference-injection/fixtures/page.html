How we split our monolith into cells

Published 2024-03-12 by the Example Corp platform team.

Example Corp serves about 40 million requests per hour from a single checkout system. In 2022 it was one deployment; after three region-wide incidents the team partitioned customers into 12 independent cells, each with its own stack, so a failure reaches at most one twelfth of customers.

The approach worked because the team was 60 engineers, the data model partitions cleanly by customer, and a thin routing layer was acceptable to the business. Cross-customer reports became harder and needed a separate pipeline.

One later service was moved back to a shared deployment because the cell overhead exceeded its benefit at low traffic.
